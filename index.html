The toolkit

Six techniques — set up when you're calm, one tap when you're not.

📞

Reach out

Pre-configure trusted contacts. A single tap sends a predefined message when you need immediate support.

⏩

Play the tape forward

Review your personal notes on the realities of a relapse. Ground yourself in long-term consequences over short-term relief.

🌊

Urge surfing

A guided breathing exercise designed to help you ride the wave of a craving until it naturally subsides.

🛟

Escape plan

Quickly access your documented strategies for leaving uncomfortable situations or environments safely.

🚫

Never romanticize

Read realistic accounts of past struggles that you wrote during moments of clarity to combat euphoric recall.

⏱

Timer + real action

Start a 15-minute delay timer paired with a mandatory distraction task to disrupt impulsive behaviors.
